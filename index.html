Sunday Funday

Your weekly slate of NFL games, ranked by what you have riding on each one.

Contact

Questions, bugs or ideas: email eric.t.tysinger@gmail.com. We usually reply within a couple of days.

Getting started

  • You need a Sleeper fantasy football account. In the Sleeper app, your username is under Account (bottom-right).
  • Pick your team and rivals, enter your Sleeper username, choose your leagues, then answer a few quick "Would you rather" questions. Your ranked slate appears right after.

Common questions

"We couldn't find that Sleeper username." Check the spelling against the username shown under Account in Sleeper (not your display name).

"That account has no NFL leagues this season." Sunday Funday reads the current season's leagues. Leagues show up once you've joined them in Sleeper.

What's a unit (u)? Your standard bet size. If you usually bet $10, then 1u = $10. If you don't bet, think of 1u as $10. It's just a benchmark for comparing what matters to you. Sunday Funday only tracks bets you've placed elsewhere; it doesn't take bets.

How are games ranked? Tap the ⓘ next to "This week" in the app.

How do I reset everything? Scroll to the bottom of the main screen and tap Start over. This deletes everything the app saved on your phone.
