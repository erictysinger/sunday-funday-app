Privacy policy

Effective November 11, 2026

Sunday Funday ("the app") is made by Eric Tysinger. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you have. The short version: there are no accounts, your setup stays on your phone, and the only thing we collect is anonymous usage data, which you can turn off.

Information that stays on your phone

Your favorite team and rivals, your "Would you rather" answers, your Sleeper username, the leagues you choose, and any bets you enter are saved only on your device. We don't have servers that store them, and we can't see them. Tapping Start over at the bottom of the main screen deletes all of it. Deleting the app does too.

Sleeper and game data

To find your leagues, the app sends the Sleeper username you enter to Sleeper's public API and downloads that account's public league, roster and projection data, along with the NFL schedule. Game times and scores come from ESPN's public scoreboard. These requests go straight from your phone to those services. Their own privacy policies apply to them: Sleeper, ESPN.

Anonymous usage data

To understand how the app is used and improve it, the app sends anonymous usage events to PostHog, our analytics provider. This includes:

  • which screens are opened, when the app is opened, and how long a visit lasts;
  • a few named actions, such as finishing setup, opening a game, adding a bet (only its size in units, not the game or pick) or removing one, turning on reminders, or sharing your slate;
  • basic device information, such as device model, iOS version, app version, language and time zone;
  • a random ID created when you install the app, so visits from the same install can be counted together;
  • approximate location (country and city) that PostHog looks up from your IP address.

We do not send your name, email, Sleeper username, league names, players, or which games you bet on. We don't use this data for advertising, don't sell it, don't share it with anyone except PostHog, which processes it on our behalf, and don't use it to track you across other apps or websites.

Turning it off: in the app, scroll to the bottom of the main screen, tap Privacy, and switch off Share anonymous usage data. Nothing more is sent from that install until you turn it back on.

Usage data is kept for as long as it's useful for improving the app. You can ask us to delete the usage data linked to your install at any time (see Contact).

Notifications

If you turn on the Sunday reminder, the app schedules it on your phone. No push server is involved. You can turn notifications off anytime in iOS Settings.

Sharing your slate

When you tap Share, the app makes an image of your slate on your phone and opens the iOS share sheet. It only goes where you choose to send it.

Children

Sunday Funday isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
